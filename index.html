FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Every new account gets free credits. A new influencer costs 1 credit, a photo 1 credit and a video 5 credits.

Will the face stay the same?

Each photo uses your influencer's signature portrait as a reference, so the identity stays consistent across scenes and outfits.

Can I post the content on social media?

Yes. Download any photo or video and post it. Many platforms ask you to label AI-generated content, so check each platform's rules.

How long does it take?

Photos take about 20 to 40 seconds. Videos take one to three minutes. You can keep working while they render.
